About Famli

Famli is a modern wealth management app that helps families know, manage and grow their wealth in one place.

Users

Financially active individuals and families who manage multiple investments and want better clarity, collaboration, and guidance.

Problem Statement

•Limited visibility of family's wealth
•Difficulty tracking spending, bills, documents and goals
•Lack of trust in financial advisors and digital tools
•Difficulty getting timely financial advice
•Financial data scattered across banks and brokers
•Limited financial knowledge for confident decisions

Key Features

Wealth Tracking
Goal Planning
Family Groups
Credit & Spending
AI Assistant
Legacy Planning
